# BRIEF: Luciana Ferreira, Psicóloga Clínica ("acolhida")

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** The user answered "use seu julgamento" to the
eight interview questions, after I proposed a default for each. Below, *Evidence* is what the user
or client actually supplied; *Authored* is my decision under that delegation.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe in three to five words, and references.**
   *Authored:* "colo, luz de fim de tarde, calma firme". Reference: the light of a room with linen
   curtains at 5 p.m.; the hand-drawn line of the client's own logo (a brain made of flowers).
   *Evidence:* the user sent three layout references (a medical landing page, an aesthetics clinic, a
   psychologist template) and a lilac palette (paletadecores.com/cores/lilas).

2. **The scroll journey, in their words.**
   *Authored:* recognition ("what I feel has a name"), whom she cares for, who Luciana is, the Sala
   Lilás trajectory, neuromodulation, how it works and where, the invitation by WhatsApp.

3. **The energy curve.** *Authored:* calm throughout, one stronger moment at the Sala Lilás
   trajectory. Visitors may arrive fragile; motion must never startle.

4. **Feeling, stage by stage, and the one moment.**
   *Evidence (client's own words):* when someone first contacts her, they should feel "que a busca
   terminou. Que ela encontrou cuidado e parceria."
   *Authored:* the remembered moment is the page turning lilac when it tells the Sala Lilás story.

5. **One thing no other site does.** *Authored:* the flower-brain from the logo is drawn by the
   visitor's own scroll. Each chapter adds strokes; it is only complete at the invitation, where it
   breathes slowly and offers a guided breath before the visitor writes.

6. **Distance from premium-minimal.** *Authored:* editorial and warm. Large serif type, magazine
   breathing room, close to minimal but with feeling.

7. **One unbroken world, or distinct scenes?** *Authored:* distinct chapters. A continuous camera
   flight does not belong in a therapist's practice.

8. **Assets.** *Evidence:* logo (raster line drawing), CV, eight-plus photos of Luciana, Instagram
   post art. *Authored:* **no generated imagery**. Only real photographs she approved; the logo is
   vectorised from her own file. No video exists.

## Grammar, score and signature

- **Grammar:** editorial chapters (printed-magazine sequence). Navigation: a quiet chapter index
  (desktop) plus the growing flower emblem. Hero device: layered arch (four planes). Close: a
  breathing invitation that holds.
- **Signature move:** scroll-drawn flower-brain emblem, completed only at the invitation, then a
  tap-to-breathe guide on the completed flower.

| Act | Device family | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Abertura | parallax layers | Depth without spectacle: atmosphere, flower, photo, foreground sprigs move at different rates |
| 2 Nome | pin + kinetic type | The frame holds still while each word arrives; being named needs stillness |
| 3 Quem cuida | reveal (clip-path wipe) | Uncovering is the meaning: who is behind the care |
| 4 Sala Lilás | colour takeover (pinned, longest) | The peak: the brand colour gets its reason |
| 5 Neuromodulação | pan (horizontal) | Side-by-side reads as options, not argument |
| 6 Como e onde | progress stack | Steps fill in order; clarity, no surprise |
| 7 Convite | pointer / interactive | The page stops moving and responds: breathe, then write |

Device families: parallax, pin/kinetic, reveal, colour takeover, pan, progress, interactive (7).
No family twice in a row. No `scrub` video.
